the thoughts that inspired this

I've realized (from personal experience too) that most of our generation is addicted to social media, in the sense that we can’t seem to healthily put it down. Hours go by like minutes and our weekly screentime spent scrolling mindlessly is well into double digits. This amount of consuming, combined with a constant flow of information and worldly issues, starts to make some of our generational overstimulation, attention, and emotional sensitivity issues make some more sense.

This site is a small archive of a few things I've wondered when it comes to the consequences of our chronic online-ness. It was cool to do some light research on how these things actually affect us, as well as surfing the internet for random commentary from people who are realizing the negative effects of constantly being online and want to actually do something to break it.
