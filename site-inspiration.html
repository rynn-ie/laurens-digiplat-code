the thoughts that inspired this

If you unglue your face from your phone for a day you’ll see just how many more people have their faces glued to their phones

Realizing that most of our generation is addicted to social media, in the sense that people can’t put it down, starts to make a lot of our generational mental and social issues make a lot more sense.
What substance do we gain from our addiction? We have no privacy, we lack connection, we have more health issues than ever, nothing on the internet feels real anymore, we’re hopeless, ect. We spend hours trying to self soothe on the internet only to end up in the same place as everyone else. It was marketed to us as more connection and a “digital escape” yet we feel more hopeless, more disconnected from reality, more isolated than ever.
It's an ugly truth, but if no one ever addresses it, our minds will continue to be owned and controlled by those manipulating our addiction and generations will continue to suffer lonelier, lazier, and stupider than those before us
